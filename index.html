Frequently Asked Questions

1 What cleaning services do you provide?
We offer residential, commercial, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, carpet and upholstery cleaning, window cleaning, and more.
2 Are your cleaning products safe for children and pets?
Yes, we use eco-friendly and non-toxic cleaning products that are safe for your family and pets.
3 Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?
No, we bring all the necessary supplies and equipment. However, if you have specific preferences, we’re happy to use your products upon request.
4 What areas do you serve?
We serve Selangor, Cyberjaya,Putrajaya and KlangValley .
5 Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?
Yes, all our team members are fully insured and have undergone thorough background checks.
6 How do I book a cleaning service?
You can book online through our website, call us at [phone number], or email us at [email address].
7 How far in advance do I need to book?
While same-day services may be available, we recommend booking at least 24-48 hours in advance.
8 Can I schedule recurring cleaning services?
Yes, we offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly cleaning plans.
9 What is your cancellation policy?
Please notify us at least [timeframe, e.g., 24 hours] before your scheduled appointment to avoid cancellation fees.
10 What happens if I need to reschedule my appointment?
Simply contact us, and we’ll work with you to find a convenient time.
11 How long does a cleaning session take?
The duration depends on the size and condition of your space. We’ll provide an estimated time during booking.
12Do you offer customized cleaning packages?
Yes, we tailor our services to meet your specific needs and preferences.
13 Will the same cleaner come every time?
For recurring services, we try to assign the same cleaner or team for consistency.
14 Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
It’s up to you! You can be present or provide access to your home or office if you’re unavailable.
15 Do you clean after events or parties?
Yes, we provide post-event cleaning services for homes and venues.
16 How much do your services cost?
Pricing depends on the type of service, size of the space, and specific requirements. Contact us for a free quote!
17 What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and online payments.
18 Are there any hidden fees?
No, our pricing is transparent, and we’ll provide a detailed breakdown before service.
19 Do you offer discounts or promotions?
Yes, we frequently offer discounts for first-time customers, referrals, and recurring services. Check our website for current offers.
20 What if I’m not satisfied with the cleaning?
Customer satisfaction is our priority. If you’re not happy, let us know within [timeframe, e.g., 24 hours], and we’ll make it right.
